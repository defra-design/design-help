About Design help

The Defra design team has moved to a management model where resource managers support line management. This has improved administration and the smooth running of core management responsibilities.

At the same time, it can leave a gap in design-focused support. Things like design critiques, design tooling help and longer-term coaching are now more dependent on the design community supporting itself.

User need

Designers need a simple way to find and offer support to each other, without relying only on informal networks or existing personal contacts.

Why this matters

Our team has a lot of experience and knowledge to share. This tool is intended to prompt people to both find help and offer help, with managers supporting the process.

What this first version is for

This is a first iteration. We can keep it, adapt it, improve it, or stop using it based on feedback and evidence of what works.

The intent is that everyone takes an active role in helping each other in our profession.

  • finding a file or piece of code
  • getting feedback on an idea
  • support with design tooling and critiques
  • career planning and coaching

Think about two things:

  • what you can offer
  • what you would like help with

If you find something that doesn’t work or a bug, please let your line manager know.
